# Additional places data

Source: Overture Maps Foundation, https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/ .
Downloaded 27 October 2026 with the official `overturemaps` client. The retained
source-record metadata is in `public/data/mumbai/place-provenance.json.gz`, keyed
by the corresponding `overture/<GERS ID>` in the runtime facility data.
